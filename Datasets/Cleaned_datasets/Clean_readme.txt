Summary of Cleaned Datasets:
This directory contains structured datasets with descriptive statistics and exploratory data analysis (EDA) results, categorized as follows:

Descriptive_Statistics_All_Dfs: Combined descriptive statistics summarizing all available data, including key measures such as mean, median, standard deviation, and distribution insights.

Descriptive_Statistics_by_Region: Aggregated descriptive statistics for five global regions, providing regional comparisons of life expectancy, GDP per capita, health expenditure, and other key indicators.

EDA_Datasets:

1.  Combined descriptive statistics for four different income classes (low, lower-middle, upper-middle, and high-income countries).
2.  Analysis of top and bottom grossing countries based on GDP per capita and life expectancy, highlighting disparities and trends.

World_Regions: Contains datasets categorized by different regions and sub-regions, facilitating detailed regional and cross-regional analysis.

These datasets serve as a foundation for understanding global health and economic trends, aiding in further analysis and visualization.
